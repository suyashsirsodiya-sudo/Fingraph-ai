System Limitations & Epistemic Governance
Methodological constraints, epistemic guardrails, and regulatory disclosures
Epistemic Hierarchy of Financial Claims
Governance Standard
Reported Fact Primary Quoted Evidence

Directly verified against external primary source filings, regulatory disclosures (SEC 10-K/8-K), or wire reports. Accompanied by exact quote spans and source URL.

Observed Market Movement Point-in-Time Metric

Empirical historical price, yield, or volatility observation from financial providers. Strictly labelled as consistent/not consistent with shock, never treated as proof of causation.

AI Inference Probabilistic Propagation

Reasoned structural link derived from knowledge graph traversal and input mechanism (cost, demand, supply). Always annotated with confidence score (0.00–1.00) and uncertainty sentence.

Hypothesis Simulated Assumption

User-configured scenario perturbation in the Scenario Lab. Must always carry the explicit banner: "Scenario Analysis — Not a Price Forecast".
